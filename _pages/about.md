Xiaolong (Andy) Wang is currently the Head of Deep Learning R&D Group in GCDO at [IBM Silicon Valley Lab](https://www.ibm.com/us-en/?ar=1). 

## Professional Experience
Previously, he was Research Manager and Senior Staff Research Scientist at AI R&D center in [Samsung Research America (SRA)](https://www.sra.samsung.com).

## Education
He completed his Ph.D. in Computer Science at the Department of CIS at the [University of Delaware (UDEL)](https://www.udel.edu/), where he worked as a research assistant at the VIMS Lab under the supervision of [Prof. Chandra Kambhamettu](https://www.eecis.udel.edu/~chandra) from 2012 to 2015. He received his M.S. degree from [West Virginia University (WVU)](https://www.wvu.edu/) in 2012.

## Research Collaborations
Throughout his career, he has collaborated with notable researchers including [Dr. Nick Cassimatis](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nickcassimatis/), [Jon Currey](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joncurrey/), [Dr. Michele Merler](https://researcher.watson.ibm.com/researcher/view.php?person=us-mimerler), and [Dr. Noel C. Codella](https://www.linkedin.com/in/noel-c-f-codella-ph-d-1b1b1723/).

- Machine Learning Infrastructure
- Biometrics
- Bioinformatics

## Publications and Recognition
He has published over 40 papers in prestigious conferences and journals. He is the recipient of the Test of Time Award at WACV 2025.

## Professional Service
He is a member of IEEE, ACM, and the IEEE Computer Society. He serves as an area chair for conferences including NeurIPS 2025, NeurIPS 2024, MM 2012, MM 2013, and others.
